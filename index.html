How it works

From the classroom to a
parent's phone

Four simple steps, with the teacher in control the whole way.

🎙️

Teach as usual

A small BrightPath pendant worn by the teacher records the lesson.

✨

AI summarizes

The recording is transcribed, turned into a lesson summary, and the audio is deleted.

✍️

Teacher stays in control

The teacher can edit or archive any summary at any time.

🔔

Parents are notified

Parents get a notification and can read the lesson in the app.
