Why BrightPath

"What did you learn today?" "Nothing." Parents want to help, but they rarely know what happened in class. BrightPath closes that gap between school and home.

Stay in the loop

See today's lessons, upcoming quizzes and school events in one place.

Help at home

A weekly report shows what your child learned and simple ways to support them.

Less work for teachers

Lessons are summarized automatically. Teachers just review and publish.

How it works

From the classroom to a parent's phone, with the teacher in control.

1

Teach as usual

A small BrightPath pendant worn by the teacher records the lesson.

2

AI summarizes

The recording is transcribed, turned into a lesson summary draft, and the audio is deleted.

3

Teacher reviews

The teacher checks and edits the summary before it goes out.

4

Parents are notified

Parents get a notification and can read the lesson in the app.

Safe by design

Audio is not kept
